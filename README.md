Welcome to **Hawks** — a community for people interested in **technology, design, business, creativity, engineering, innovation, and more.**

Hawks isn't just a community to join. **It's a community to contribute to.**

## 🤝 Community Guidelines

* Be respectful and supportive of other members.
* Share useful **resources, internships, hackathons, events, scholarships, jobs, and opportunities**.
* Help others, ask questions, share knowledge, and collaborate.
* Avoid spam, harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and unnecessary self-promotion.
* Give proper credit when sharing or using someone else's work.
* Keep shared information relevant and useful to the community.

## 🦅 Becoming a Hawks Admin

Being an admin is a **responsibility, not just a title.**

To become and remain a Hawks Admin, you should:

* Consistently contribute to the community.
* Share relevant opportunities and information in a domain you're interested in.
* Help members and participate in discussions.
* Support Hawks initiatives and collaborations.
* Maintain a respectful and productive environment.

**Admin roles may be reviewed or removed if there is prolonged inactivity, misuse of permissions, or repeated violation of community guidelines.**

## 🏷️ Hawks Tag

Use **#Hawks / Hawks** when participating in hackathons, events, projects, or sharing achievements.

**Share your achievements with the community! 🏆**

## 🏗️ Community Structure

* **Playground** — Discussions, networking, ideas & collaboration.
* **Ambassadors** — Microsoft Student Ambassadors.
* **Resources** — Learning resources & opportunities.
* **MLSC NMIET Pune** — For students enrolled at PCET's NMIET.
